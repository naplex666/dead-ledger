# Product steering — Dead Ledger

Dead Ledger resurrects the paper *buku kas* (cash & kasbon ledger book) used
by Indonesian warung owners and tradespeople. Every suggestion must serve a
non-technical user on a low-end Android phone.

Rules:
- The three questions the product answers, in priority order:
  1. Who still owes me, and how much?
  2. How much money came in and went out?
  3. What is my balance?
- Amounts are Indonesian Rupiah, whole numbers, formatted `Rp1.250.000`.
- No accounts, no server, no network dependency. Data lives in the browser.
- Any new feature must be usable without instructions. If it needs a manual,
  simplify it instead.
- UI copy is plain English (hackathon submission language) with Indonesian
  domain words kept where they carry meaning: *kasbon* (tab/credit owed),
  *warung* (small shop).
- The Halloween theme must enhance function (contrast, overdue warnings,
  settled = ghosted). Never add spooky decoration that slows the user down.
